# Known Limitations (v1)

- Visuals use placeholder geometry (no final sprite atlas/animation sheets yet).
- AI is intentionally lightweight and may produce occasional unrealistic movement.
- Ball out-of-play handling is minimal and simplified.
- No online PvP or replay viewer UI in v1.
- Deck builder editing is type-count based (not per-card drag/drop UX).
- Performance warning remains for Phaser bundle size in production build.

# ✅ 1. Core Fantasy (What Makes Your Game Fun?)

Your game’s “fun” comes from three emotional pillars:

### **1) Tactical Mastery**

You’re not controlling players directly — you’re **commanding**. This feels smart, strategic, and satisfying.

### **2) Team Growth**

Your players (cards) improve over time, making every match feel meaningful.

### **3) Creative Coaching**

Players get to shape a playstyle using:

* action commands
* team command cards
* formations
* evolving abilities

This creates a unique coaching-RPG fantasy.

# 🏈 2. Core Gameplay Loop

A good loop is essential. Here’s one tailored to your idea:

**MATCH → REWARDS → PLAYER GROWTH → DECK TUNING → MATCH**

Let’s break it down:

![Image](https://www.iconicedinburgh.co.uk/cdn/shop/products/il_fullxfull.2142930750_gin4_1024x1024%402x.jpg?v=1753476807)

![Image](https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/43747950/file/still-0aeab1eaeefcb14ffce8ccbdce8947bf.png?resize=400x0)

![Image](https://img.craftpix.net/2019/06/Free-Battle-Location-Top-Down-Game-Tileset-Pack3.jpg)

### **During the match**

* You draw action cards (pass, shoot, dribble, press, mark, rush)
* You choose WHEN to play the card based on the situation
* Some actions require direction (shoot, pass)
* Your team follows basic AI but reacts to your commands

### **After the match**

* Cards (players) gain XP
* Some stats increase
* You might unlock new perks or traits
* You tweak your deck of team command cards

This loop naturally creates **progression + mastery + anticipation**.

# 🎮 3. The Command System (Your Unique Selling Point)

This is where your game really shines.

## **A. Individual Action Cards**

Examples:

* **Pass (direction)**
* **Shoot (direction)**
* **Dribble**
* **Through Ball**
* **Tackle**
* **Press**
* **Mark / Cover**
* **Switch Sides**

Each card has:

* **Cost** (stamina, cooldown, etc.)
* **Success chance** (affected by player stats)
* **Risk/reward** curve

This makes the game feel like a **football tactics card battler in real time**.

## **B. Team Command Cards**

(consume on use, limited per match)

Examples:

* Park the Bus
* All Out Attack
* Fast Counter
* High Press
* Slow Build-Up
* Wing Overload
* Last 10 Minutes Fury

These cards create **big momentum swings** and change the match flow instantly.

## Why this works

You avoid “twitch skill” and focus on **smart decision-making**, similar to games like
Sensible Soccer but with the modern strategy focus of a card battler.

# ⚙️ 4. Player Cards & Stats (The RPG Layer)

Each player card has RPG stats such as:

* Pace
* Passing
* Shooting
* Strength
* Positioning
* Decision Making
* Stamina
* Tackle
* Vision

### **Top Limit System (SMART CHOICE)**

Each card can’t max everything — players must specialize.

This makes your team feel:

* **custom built**
* **unique**
* **strategic**

This is also great for *long-term engagement*.

### **Traits/Perks**

Add personality and combos:

* “Precision Passer”
* “Fox in the Box”
* “Engine”
* “Counter Attack Specialist”
* “Long Shot Threat”
* “Pressing Monster”

Perks make players feel like **RPG characters**, not stats.

# 🧠 5. AI Behavior (Crucial for Fun)

Your AI needs to support the command system.

### The AI should:

* Maintain formation
* Seek intelligent passing lanes
* Respond to your commands FAST
* Understand momentum (pressure vs retreat)
* Prioritize strong players automatically

### Avoid:

* Too much autonomy
* Too much randomness
* Players ignoring commands

Fun comes from **predictable foundations + strategic surprises**.

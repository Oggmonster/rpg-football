# ⭐ 1. PLAYER CARD PROGRESSION (core RPG layer)

This is the emotional backbone of the game — players get attached to their cards.

## **1.1 XP Gain**

Player cards earn XP from:

* Minutes played
* Match result
* Individual performance (passes, tackles, shots)
* Tactical success (traits triggered, command synergy)
* Match rating
* Momentum swings caused by that player

Each role generates XP differently (strikers via shots; midfielders via passes; defenders via tackles).

## **1.2 Level System**

Levels 1 → N based on rarity:

| Rarity    | Level Cap | Notes                        |
| --------- | --------- | ---------------------------- |
| Common    | 10        | Light growth                 |
| Uncommon  | 15        | Moderate growth              |
| Rare      | 20        | Good growth                  |
| Epic      | 25        | Strong growth                |
| Legendary | 30        | Big growth                   |
| Mythic    | 40        | Signature abilities unlocked |

Higher rarity = better caps + traits + perk slots.

## **1.3 Stat Growth**

At level-up:

* +Stat points distributed using **role-weighted curves**
* 70% predictable, 30% random within the cap range

**Growth caps** stop universal max builds and force identity.

## **1.4 Perks & Traits Unlocking**

Every X levels, a player unlocks:

* **Perk slot** (choose from pool)
* **Advanced trait** (role-based)

Example unlock path:

* Lv 5 → +1 Perk Slot
* Lv 10 → New Trait (e.g., "Box-to-Box Engine")
* Lv 15 → Specialization Path (choose 1 of 3 mini-class options)
* Lv 20 → Signature Move (Rare+)

# ⭐ 2. TEAM COMMAND CARD PROGRESSION

(*Your macro-tactics grow over time.*)

Team command cards (All Out Attack, Park the Bus, High Press, etc.) have their own leveling.

## **2.1 How Team Cards Gain XP**

* Using them in matches
* Winning with them active
* Momentum swings triggered during their effect

## **2.2 Team Card Levels Improve:**

* Duration
* Power
* Cooldown
* Passive buffs
* Clash-resistance (less penalty when misused)

Example:
**Fast Counter Lv1 → Lv5**

* Duration: 4s → 7s
* Through-ball bonus: +10% → +25%
* Sprint buff: +5% → +15%

## **2.3 Team Command Rarity**

Team cards also have rarities, determining:

* Maximum power
* Unique tactical bonuses
* Synergy with certain player traits

Example:
Legendary *High Press Arsenal*:
“Every successful press grants +1% momentum.”

(*Your account-level progression.*)

Your personal level unlocks global features.

## **3.1 Manager XP Comes From:**

* Matches played
* Winning streaks
* Completing challenges
* Using diverse tactics
* Seasonal objectives

## **3.2 Unlock Rewards**

* New formations
* New action cards
* New team command slots
* Training modes
* Access to new leagues
* Cosmetic upgrades (stadiums, kits)

Example:

* Manager Lv 3 → Unlock 4-3-3 Formation

# ⭐ 4. CLUB FACILITIES (META PROGRESSION)

This is where the long-term RPG strategy lives — building your club.

Facilities include:

### **4.1 Training Center**

* Boosts XP earned by players
* Unlocks “Daily Training Drills” (mini challenges)
* Higher levels let you reroll stat growth on level-up

### **4.2 Medical Center**

* Reduces injury chance
* Shortens downtime
* Increases stamina regen between matches

### **4.3 Scouting Department**

* Improves quality of new player cards
* Better packs
* Higher rarity chance
* Unlocks specific role scouts (Striker Scout, Defender Scout, etc.)

### **4.4 Tactics Lab**

* Reduces cooldown on Team Commands
* Unlocks unique tactical variants (e.g., “Ultra Fast Counter”)

### **4.5 Stadium**

* Affects match rewards
* Boosts home momentum
* Aesthetic upgrades

Each facility has level progression using resources (coins, upgrade items).

# ⭐ 5. SEASON & LEAGUE ADVANCEMENT

(*The main campaign loop.*)

Your team competes in:

## **5.1 Divisions / Leagues**

* Division 10 → Division 1
* Promotion on win streaks
* Demotion on losing streaks
* Higher divisions = stronger AI + better rewards

## **5.2 Seasonal Structure**

Each season lasts X days/weeks.

Progress resets:

* League rank
* Seasonal rewards
* Challenges

Does NOT reset:

* Cards
* Levels
* Club upgrades
* Tactics

## **5.3 Seasonal Rewards**

* Exclusive team command cards
* Legendary player cards
* Unique stadium cosmetics
* Title badges

## **5.4 Seasonal Events**

Rotating modes:

* “Counter-Attack Cup”
* “Wing Play Challenge”
* “Defensive Mastery Week”

Each mode boosts XP for relevant cards or unlocks exclusive perks.

# ⭐ 6. ECONOMY SYSTEM (Currencies + Flow)

To keep it elegant and manageable:

## **6.1 Currencies**

### **Soft Currency (Coins)**

* Match rewards
* Daily quests
* Training challenges
* League advancement

Used for:

* Upgrading facilities
* Buying basic packs
* Rerolling stat-ups

### **Hard Currency (Gems)**

(Optional if free-to-play)

Used for:

* Premium packs
* Instant facility finishes
* Cosmetic-only content
* Extra perk slots (limited)

### **Energy/Stamina**

NOT for real-world timers — only applies to players in matches.

### **Training Tokens**

Used to upgrade individual player stats within cap limits.

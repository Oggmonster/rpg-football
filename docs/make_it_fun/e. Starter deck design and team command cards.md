# 🅔 — **STARTER DECK DESIGN + FIRST 10 TEAM COMMAND CARDS**

Your starter deck is meant to teach:

* the **core command rhythm**
* the difference between **action cards** and **team tactical cards**
* the **risk/reward** nature of your game
* how to **time** and **chain** commands

Let’s break it down:

---

# ⭐ 1. STARTER ACTION DECK (the default 8 cards)

These are the bread-and-butter commands players will use constantly.
They are unlimited use with cooldowns.

### **1) Pass**

* Offense only
* Short pass to nearest viable teammate
* Cooldown: 3 sec
* Very low risk
* Scales with Passing + Vision

### **2) Shoot**

* Offense only
* Directional input
* Cooldown: 7 sec
* Medium risk
* Chance-based on Shooting + Decision Making

### **3) Dribble**

* Ball carrier attempts evasive moves for 2 sec
* Cooldown: 6 sec
* Medium–high risk
* Boosts dribble retention but increases turnover chance if tackled

### **4) Through Ball**

* Forward-biased pass into space
* Requires directional cone
* Cooldown: 8 sec
* High risk / high reward

### **5) Press**

* Defense only
* Closest 2–3 players collapse toward the ball
* Cooldown: 6 sec
* Risk: Opens gaps

### **6) Tackle**

* Force immediate challenge
* Cooldown: 5 sec
* Risk: foul chance + momentum loss if failed

### **7) Mark / Cover**

* Assign a nearby opponent for tight marking
* Cooldown: 4 sec
* Low risk

### **8) Switch Play**

* Directional long-ball switch to opposite wing
* Cooldown: 10 sec
* Risk: interception chance unless Vision is high

**These 8 cards + cooldowns = the core language of your game.**

# ⭐ 2. STARTER TEAM COMMAND DECK (first 10 tactical cards)

These are **consumable**, **impactful**, and **limited per match**.

* Effect
* Duration
* Momentum impact
* Counterplay

# 🔥 TEAM COMMAND #1 — **ALL OUT ATTACK**

**Offense power spike**

**Effect:**

* Fullbacks push high
* Passing + Shooting + Run Speed +10%
* Defensive stats −10%

**Duration:** 20 sec
**Momentum:** + small positive swing
**Risk:** Leaves you wide open to counters
**Synergy:** Strikers, CAMs, wingers

---

# 🛡 TEAM COMMAND #2 — **PARK THE BUS**

**Defensive turtle stance**

**Effect:**

* Backline compresses
* Tackling, Interception +15%
* Passing & Dribbling −10%

**Duration:** 25 sec
**Momentum:** Slight negative (you’re conceding space)
**Risk:** You can’t counter while in this state
**Synergy:** CBs, CDMs

---

# ⚡ TEAM COMMAND #3 — **FAST COUNTER**

**Explosive transition attack**

**Effect:**

* When you win the ball, forwards instantly sprint
* Through Ball success +25%
* Sprint speed +15% for 4 sec after ball recovery

**Duration:** 15 sec
**Momentum:** Medium positive if triggered during recovery
**Risk:** If you lose the ball again, you’re very exposed
**Synergy:** Pacey wingers, CAMs

---

# 🌪 TEAM COMMAND #4 — **HIGH PRESS**

**Aggressive forward pressing**

**Effect:**

* Front 3–4 players push high
* Press & Tackle cards get −30% cooldown
* Defense line moves up 5–10 yards

**Duration:** 20 sec
**Momentum:** Strong positive if successful
**Risk:** Long-ball vulnerability
**Synergy:** Strikers, high stamina players

---

# 🧊 TEAM COMMAND #5 — **SLOW BUILD-UP**

**Safe possession play**

**Effect:**

* Passing success +10%
* Player spacing widens
* Opponents press less effectively
* Pace −10%

**Duration:** 22 sec
**Momentum:** Slight positive if you maintain long pass chains
**Risk:** Harder to create sudden chances
**Synergy:** Playmakers, high Vision players

---

# 🌈 TEAM COMMAND #6 — **WING OVERLOAD**

**Attack one flank hard**

**Effect:**

* 2–3 players shift toward selected wing
* Cross success +20%
* Opposite wing becomes weaker defensively

**Duration:** 18 sec
**Momentum:** Neutral → positive if you generate crosses
**Risk:** Leaves one side exposed
**Synergy:** Wingers, wingbacks

---

# 🚧 TEAM COMMAND #7 — **MIDFIELD LOCKDOWN**

**Dominate central control**

**Duration:** 20 sec
